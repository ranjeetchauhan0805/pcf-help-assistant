WHO WE ARE
About
Positive Change
A registered NGO committed to empowering lives through Education, Health and Community Development since 2025.
🎯
Our Mission
To empower underprivileged communities through sustainable programs in education, healthcare, women empowerment, and youth development across India.
🌟
Our Vision
A society where every individual has equal access to education, healthcare, and opportunities to lead a dignified and fulfilling life.
OUR STORY
Creating Impact
Since 2025
Positive Change Foundation was established on
1st November 2025
by
Mehmood Hakimi
with a vision to bridge the gap between the privileged and underprivileged sections of society.
Starting from Mumbai, we have grown into a movement that works across India through programs in education, healthcare, women empowerment, youth development, and legal aid.
